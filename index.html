Buggin’ Out!

Some ants patiently waiting for the game.

A multiplayer bug-catching game where you explore, compete and care for bugs in a whimsical, hand-crafted world.

  • Multiplayer: Online sessions for up to 12 players.
  • Exploration: Multiple biomes, landmarks and NPCs to discover.
  • Creature Collection: Over 100 unique bugs to find and capture.
  • Seasons & Minigames: Seasonal events and party-style minigames.
